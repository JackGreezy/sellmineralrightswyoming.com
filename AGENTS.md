# Sell Mineral Rights Wyoming — Site-Specific Rules

This project has its own business voice and its own Thales-derived visual system. Do not import a reusable mineral-rights voice pack. Do not reuse another site's hero treatment, FAQ, related-content rail, article ending, CTA, or decorative language.

## Voice lock

The voice is a Wyoming courthouse landman writing a field memorandum for an owner. It is plainspoken, short, and concrete. It explains fee minerals, federal checkerboard ground, county records, legal descriptions, paid decimals, division orders, title gaps, leases, producing wells, spacing, and recording. It never uses first-person singular business narration, promises a highest offer, presents an instant value, or invents certainty.

## Visual lock

The reference is Thales. Preserve its Inter and Saira typography, white dual-level header, deep blue and cyan palette, editorial split hero, full-bleed media, exact three-card fields, light/dark cadence, two-column research panel, user-action band, functional horizontal story rail, sharp rectangular controls, and dense navy footer. Slug pages use one wide hero image, a broad article measure, a Wyoming curative-file FAQ, checkerboard plat related links, and a county-record closing docket. Never insert a generic rounded card stack.

## Release blockers

- The street address cannot appear as text. It may exist only in a Google Maps embed query.
- No footer thumbnails, decorative mini-images, blank images, one-pixel images, floating media glitches, emoji, or Unicode-arrow decoration.
- Every slug page has exactly one image: its hero image.
- No narrow one-character columns, vertical writing, invisible copy, weak contrast, clipped sections, or overlapping desktop header content.
- Desktop header includes real routes, the phone number, and a clear contact CTA.
- Header dropdowns open by hover and focus with no dead pointer gap. The mobile menu and every carousel arrow work.
- The contact form contains exactly Name, Email, Phone, and Message.
- Every footer contains a Google Maps embed.
- The homepage hero contains an SEO-driven H1.
- Every page retains the Thales-derived font and theme.
- Written content uses a business voice, never first-person singular narration.

The build is complete only after both machine gates, the full desktop/mobile screenshot set, the interaction checks, a production deployment, and live alias verification all pass.
